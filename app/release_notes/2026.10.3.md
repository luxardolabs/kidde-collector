# 2026.10.3 — 2026-10-22

A security update, and a health check that tells you the truth.

> Surfaces: `Collector` (the running service), `Dashboards` (the Grafana dashboards it feeds).

## IMPROVED

- **Security update** `Collector` — Closes one critical and three high-severity vulnerabilities in the components the collector uses to talk to Kidde and your database. Your Kidde sign-in is also never readable by other users on the host, and never sent anywhere but Kidde.
- **Healthy means your data is arriving** `Collector` — The container reports healthy only after a reading has actually been saved to your database. If it can't sign in to Kidde or can't save, it shows as unhealthy instead of looking fine.
- **Gentler on your Kidde account after a password change** `Collector` — If Kidde turns down your password, the collector waits before trying again, starting at a minute and growing to an hour, so a wrong password can't get your account locked. Restart after fixing it to retry straight away.
- **Recovers from a damaged sign-in file on its own** `Collector` — If the saved sign-in file is damaged, say by a crash or a full disk, the collector signs in fresh with nothing for you to delete.

## FIXED

- **Smoke and CO alarms could go missing from the dashboards while the collector looked healthy** `Dashboards` — If Kidde sent a reading in a different form than before, that detector's whole reading, alarm state included, was quietly not saved. It now shows as unhealthy and logs which reading caused it; TROUBLESHOOTING.md has the fix.
- **One bad device record no longer blanks every detector** `Dashboards` — An incomplete record from Kidde skips only that device; the rest keep reporting.
- **A detector name ending in a backslash showed up renamed** `Dashboards` — It now appears under its own name, without the backslash.
- **Your Kidde password and database token are fully hidden in the startup log** `Collector` — The log shows only that each is set: no characters, no length.
