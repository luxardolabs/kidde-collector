# 2026.09.0 — 2026-09-06

Your detectors' smoke, CO and battery warnings now show up on the dashboards, a security update for the collector's network library, and a fix for a health check that could quietly call a stalled collector healthy.

## NEW

- **Safety & Power row** — Both dashboards now show what your detectors report about safety: smoke and CO alarms, low-battery and end-of-life warnings, battery level and state, and current and peak carbon monoxide. It was always being collected, just never shown.
- **Carbon monoxide readings** — CO is now charted alongside the air-quality metrics, with a marker at the level where an alarm is required to sound, so a rising reading is obvious rather than something you have to go looking for.
- **At-a-glance alarm status** — Each detector shows OK or ALARM in green or red, so one look tells you whether anything needs attention.

## IMPROVED

- **Grafana now opens on port 3300** — The demo and dev stacks used port 3000, which almost anything else on the machine also wants. Update your bookmark; you can still pick your own port if 3300 is taken.
- **No more port clashes when starting up** — The bundled database no longer claims a port on your machine, because nothing outside the stack needed it. Starting the demo or dev stack alongside other services just works instead of failing to start.
- **Security update** — Updated the library the collector uses to talk to Kidde and to your database, closing three published vulnerabilities.
- **Reliability** — A large amount of behind-the-scenes testing and stability work, including verification against real detectors and a clean, prompt shutdown when the container is stopped.

## FIXED

- **Health check could call a stalled collector healthy** — If your machine is not on UTC, then for one hour when the clocks go back the collector could report itself healthy while it had actually stopped recording. It now measures elapsed time in a way the clock change cannot affect.
- **Blank cells in the safety table are real** — Not every detector reports every field: only battery-powered CO units report battery level and end-of-life, and only smoke-capable units report smoke alarms. An empty cell means that model does not report it, not that something is broken. Noted on the panel itself.
- **Quiet failures now get logged** — Some errors when disconnecting from the database were being discarded entirely. They are now recorded, so a problem at shutdown leaves a trace instead of vanishing.
